the game name is "Super Hoops"
I want to build a fast-paced 2D arcade basketball game in a single HTML file where you control a ball that constantly bounces around the screen using realistic gravity physics. The player steers left and right with the arrow keys or A/D, and holding Spacebar charges up a jump the longer you hold it, the higher the ball goes up into the air when you let go. The court floor has a full hardwood texture complete with court lines, key arcs, and amazing lighting.

There are super detailed  good looking basketball hoops on both the left and right sides of the screen that constantly move up and down randomly and add a flexible net that bends with a cool "SWOOSH!" particle animation whenever you score. Floating around the screen and trying to blok you is a sequence of 20 unique boss monsters, starting with an easy slime and getting progressively harder up to cyber-skulls and dragons. The bosses have animated arms and hands that actively wave around to block your shots. Every time you score a basket, you deal damage to the current boss, increase your combo streak, and add 4 seconds back to your countdown timer (capped at 30 seconds max) they start off super easy to score on and progresivlt get more difficult.

To keep gameplay exciting, power up particles spawn one at a time around the arena with distinct colors: Blue means freezes the moving hoops, Purple slows down the boss, Gold spawns multi-ball clones, green rewinds your movement, orange gives you a shield, and pink triggers double points. At the same time, red/black hazard particles continuously float around the arena that deduct points, drain jump charge, or steal time if you hit them, the higher the level your at the more particls that spawn. The game includes a modern semi-transparent HUD showing your active power-up at the bottom, score, combo streak, timer, and a boss health bar up top. It also features a full main menu with a 20-boss Bestiary, controls guide, procedural sound effects for bounces, swishes, and hits, plus screen shake when you land a big shot. add sound, brightness, and setings to the menu.
